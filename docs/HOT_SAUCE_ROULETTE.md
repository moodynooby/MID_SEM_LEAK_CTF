# 🌶️ Hot Sauce Roulette & Safety Protocols Guide
> **Case File: The Mid-Sem Leak** | IEEE AUSB CTF (28 Oct 2026, AU Labs 207 & 208)  
> **Gamification Leads:** @tanishshahh & @VP1107 | **Tech Leads:** Manas (@moodynooby) & Krish (@Krish-452)

## 1. Concept & In-Game Mechanic
During designated high-stakes competition intervals, teams stuck on complex forensics or cryptography challenges can trigger a physical lifeline:
- **The Challenge:** One team member steps up to the official volunteer station and consumes one level spoonful of commercial hot sauce (or dietary alternative).
- **The In-Game Reward:** Upon verified consumption by an IEEE volunteer marshal, the Tech Leads grant the team **1 unpenalized hint docket** for their requested challenge with **0 point deduction** (saving 30–80 points).
- **Wheel Trigger:** Landing on the **🌶️ Hot Sauce Shot (20% weight)** on the Big-Screen Projector Spin Wheel (`/spin-wheel/`) also awards this lifeline opportunity immediately.

- **Window 1 (Post-Vanishing Window):** `4:45 PM – 4:55 PM` (Right after the 15-minute vanishing bonus challenge closes).
- **Window 2 (Lightning Round Surge):** `5:00 PM – 5:10 PM` (During the double-point speed round).

## 3. Station Location & Physical Setup
- **Location:** Front demonstration table of **GICT Lab 207** (well-lit, within line of sight of Tech Lead console).
- **Safety Equipment on Station:**
  - 1x Box of Powder-Free Nitrile Gloves (worn at all times by volunteer handlers).
  - 1x Box of Disposable Single-Use Birchwood Spoons (never re-dipped).
  - 2x Rolls of Heavy-Duty Paper Towels & Napkins.
  - 4x 1-Liter Jugs of Cold Whole Milk / Fresh Yogurt (casein instantly neutralizes capsaicin).
  - 50x 200ml Disposable Paper Water Cups.
  - 1x Dedicated Trash Receptacle for used spoons and napkins.

---

## 4. Sauce Specifications & Food Safety
- **Product:** Certified commercial food-grade mild-to-medium hot sauce (e.g. Huy Fong Sriracha or Tabasco Mild Garlic, approximately **5,000–10,000 Scoville Heat Units**).
- **Standard Dose:** Exactly one single level teaspoon (approx. 5 ml) per volunteer participant.
- **Expiry & Seal:** Brand-new sealed bottle opened on the day of the event (28 Oct 2026).

---

## 5. Voluntary Opt-In & Medical / Allergy Protocols
- **Strictly Opt-In:** Participation is 100% voluntary. No participant may be pressured or compelled to consume hot sauce.
- **Allergy & Intolerance Alternatives:**
  - Any participant with acid reflux, gastric sensitivity, capsicum allergy, or personal dietary preference is provided with a safe non-chili alternative:
    - **Alternative A:** 1 Handful of Japanese Roasted Wasabi Peas.
    - **Alternative B:** 1 Piece of Mega-Sour Citrus Candy (Black Death / Warheads).
  - Completing either alternative qualifies for the exact same unpenalized hint docket reward.
- **Cooling Down:** Immediately provide the participant with a cup of cold whole milk or yogurt and water.
