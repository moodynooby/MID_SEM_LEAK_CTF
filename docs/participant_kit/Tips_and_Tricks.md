# 💡 Investigator Tips & Strategy Guide
## Case File: The Mid-Sem Leak — IEEE AUSB

Tips to maximize your team's score and solve the case efficiently:

---

### 1. Teamwork & Role Delegation (Teams of 2–4)
- **Don't all look at the same screen:** Divide and conquer.
  - **Teammate 1:** Handles forensics files (EXIF, hex editing, Wireshark).
  - **Teammate 2:** Handles web inspection & log grepping.
  - **Teammate 3:** Tackles cryptography and cipher decoding in CyberChef.
- **Act II Dual-Route Strategy:** Since Act II has two routes (Log Analysis and Web Source), pick the one your team feels most confident with!

---

### 2. Time Management & Timed Events
- **Keep an eye on the clock:**
  - **4:35 PM:** The 15-minute Vanishing Bonus challenge will pop up. Have one person immediately download and inspect it while others continue the main acts.
  - **5:00 PM:** The 10-minute Lightning Round is worth double points! Drop non-urgent tasks for 5 minutes to grab those quick points.
  - **5:15 PM:** Don't panic when the scoreboard goes dark. Keep submitting flags right up until 5:30 PM.

---

### 3. Note-Taking & Evidence Correlation
- Keep a shared scratchpad (Notepad, VS Code, or Google Doc) open between team members.
- Record every clue:
  - Timestamps (e.g. `21:14:02`)
  - IP addresses (e.g. `10.207.x.x`)
  - Usernames and handles (e.g. `@ghost_in_the_lab`)
  - MAC addresses
- You will need to correlate all of these in **Act V** to name the fictional culprit!

---

### 4. Smart Use of Hints
- Hints only deduct a small number of points (-20 to -50 pts).
- If your team is stuck for more than 10 minutes on a challenge, **take the hint!** Gaining 450 points after a 50-point hint is far better than remaining at 0 points.
- Floor volunteers are friendly — feel free to ask them for general guidance or in-character nudges!
