# 💡 Investigator Tips & Strategy Guide
## Case File: The Mid-Sem Leak — IEEE AUSB

Tips to maximize your team's score and solve the case efficiently:

---

### 1. Teamwork & Role Delegation (Teams of 2–4)
- **Don't all look at the same screen:** Divide and conquer.
  - **Teammate 1:** Handles forensics artifacts (git reflogs, hex recovery, Wireshark PCAPs).
  - **Teammate 2:** Handles web inspection, cookie tampering, and AI prompt interaction.
  - **Teammate 3:** Tackles binary decompilation (JADX) and targeted dictionary generation for hash cracking.
- **Cross-Track Correlation:** Clues discovered in wireless DHCP logs or case briefings directly assist in building wordlists and validating suspect alibis.

---

### 2. Time Management & Competition Milestones
- **Keep an eye on the clock:**
  - **4:30 PM:** All challenge tracks unlock simultaneously. Start with quick warmup challenges (cheatsheet, PDF, fragmented chunks) to get on the scoreboard early!
  - **4:45 PM & 5:10 PM:** Watch for Intel Clue Drops broadcasted on the live projector screen. Stuck teams can utilize Spin Wheel lifelines or hints.
  - **5:15 PM:** Scoreboard Blackout! The public scoreboard freezes for the final 15 minutes of suspense. Keep submitting flags right up until 5:30 PM.

---

### 3. Note-Taking & Evidence Correlation
- Keep a shared scratchpad (Notepad, VS Code, or Google Doc) open between team members.
- Record every clue:
  - Timestamps (e.g. `21:14:02`)
  - IP addresses (e.g. `10.207.x.x`)
  - Usernames and handles (e.g. `@ghost_in_the_lab`)
  - MAC addresses
- You will need to correlate all of these across forensic tracks to uncover the culprit's motive and reconstruct the leak!

---

### 4. Smart Use of Hints
- Hints only deduct a small number of points (-20 to -50 pts).
- If your team is stuck for more than 10 minutes on a challenge, **take the hint!** Gaining 450 points after a 50-point hint is far better than remaining at 0 points.
- Floor volunteers are friendly — feel free to ask them for general guidance or in-character nudges!
